## 1. THE RESULT, AND THE THREE MEASUREMENTS THAT MAKE IT

Read these together. **Any one of them alone points the wrong way.**

| readout, identical 6000-clip test set | srcc_robust |
|---|---|
| ridge probe on stats-pooled FROZEN WavLM (untuned, α=10) | **0.6883** |
| LM free decode | **0.6986** |
| **verified-slot decode  ← HEADLINE** | **0.7246** |
| aux-head raw values (ceiling) | **0.7280** |

- The **skyline alone** says a linear probe ties the whole pipeline — the accuracy claim is dead.
- The **aux head alone** says the 8B LM is worse than one `Linear(4096→11)` on the pooled prefix.
- **Together** they say: the adapter's representation genuinely beats a linear probe by
  **+0.040** (outside the 0.017 seed spread), and *the LM was discarding that gain*.
  Verified-slot decoding recovers it — **+0.0363 over the skyline**, capturing **99.5%** of
  the aux-head ceiling.

**Defensible claim:** a learned adapter encodes acoustic measurements a linear probe on the
same frozen features cannot recover; free-running LM decoding **degrades** them; substituting
the computed value preserves them — and because the number is *computed rather than
generated*, each claim carries causal evidence by construction.

Per-feature (verified-slot, full test): snr 0.948 · srmr 0.869 · pause_count 0.638 ·
speaking_rate 0.592 · pause_rate 0.576 · f0_mean 0.383. nMAE 0.4842 (floor 0.7942).

Decomposition (matched 2000 clips): free 0.6732 | slot-free 0.6368 (**−0.036**) |
slot-verified 0.7054 (**+0.032**). Slot-free *loses*, so the gain is the **estimator swap**,
not the slot structure. This SUPERSEDES the old triple 0.6213/0.5918/0.5527 (verified worst),
which was measured with the pooling-bug-corrupted aux head — do not cite it.

## 3. ABSTENTION — the lead contribution

- **AURC gain vs random confidence — DEFINITION-EXPLICIT, corrected 2026-08-09.**
  The "+18.6% (11.7–24.0%); recoverable up to +79%" recorded here was measured with a **BINARY
  MISCOVERAGE loss** (`|err| / FEATURE_SCALES > 1.0`, the frozen Mondrian k) on the **fw2 arm**
  (`bsigma_attn_fw2_seed73`), not the arm of record. It reproduces digit-for-digit under that
  definition — it was never wrong, only underspecified.
  **On the SHIPPING arm (`bsigma_attnconcat_seed73`) with CONTINUOUS absolute-error risk:
  ill-posed +12.2% (range 6.4–18.1), robust5 +19.9%, best feature srmr +38.3%.**
  Quote those. **NEVER quote "recoverable up to +79%"**: that snr row orders ~6 error events
  (base risk 0.001 x 6000) and the recoverable-panel MEAN in the original output was literally
  `nan` — the "+79%" was quoted precisely because the mean was unquotable.
- **Mondrian/RCPS certification: 0 of 15 cells** at α=0.1, k=1.0. σ error-ranking is 0.06–0.25
  against a simulated requirement of **≈0.24–0.30** — a factor of ~1.5–2×.
  **⚠️ CONFORMAL FRAMING DROPPED 2026-08-07.** Not closable at current σ quality, and AURC
  already carries the same claim without a guarantee we cannot make (state the loss — see above). Report AURC;
  report the certification shortfall only as an honest negative. MDN is the ONE cheap shot at
  the σ gap (f0 under overlap is genuinely bimodal; a unimodal σ cannot represent it) — if it
  fails, stop here rather than spending more on conformal machinery.
- Text-level abstention works: emitted on 2827 clean clips vs **4** overlap clips.
- **BUT it is only demonstrable as a BINARY contrast.** Test mixtures 99.1% at overlap ≥0.5,
  dev 99.4%, clean 100.0% at exactly 0. "Abstains under overlap" and "abstains on mixtures"
  are observationally identical. Graded abstention needs **synthesised partial-overlap clips**
  (19 in dev, 27 in test, 2 in the 200-clip val subset — no sampling scheme rescues that).

## 4. DATA FACTS THAT CONSTRAIN CLAIMS

- **`overlap_ratio` is an input echo** — ρ 0.9999 with the mean of input channel 0. Never
  report as a predicted feature.
- **`snr_db` is INJECTED noise, controlled by construction** — ρ +0.939 with the s1clean
  noise floor, −0.021 with the speaker-to-interferer ratio. A STRENGTH (exact GT by design).
  `scripts/augment_reverb.py` would give RT60 the same status but **has never been run**.
- **9 of 11 GT features are byte-identical between each mixture clip and its `_s1clean` twin**
  (only srmr and overlap_ratio are per-signal). Effective unique GT: **13,900 / 3,000 / 3,000**.
- **pause_count is partly a length artifact** — duration alone reaches 0.422 vs the model's
  0.626. All others clean (snr 0.006, srmr 0.029).
- **f0 beats a constant predictor by +4%**, speaking_rate by +14% (free decode).
- Confounds are SMALLER than long-quoted: srmr~f0 **+0.246** (not 0.80); snr~temporal
  **≤0.011** (not 0.57–0.66) — the clean-stem measurement fix appears to have removed them.
- **Self-referential GT is empirically a non-issue**: scoring against the instrument CSV gives
  numbers identical to scoring against parsed target prose. `--gt_from_csv` is now the default.
